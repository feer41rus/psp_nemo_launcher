# 1.2.3 — Splash screens and sounds
- Splash screen at power-on: "Welcome! Your PSP. The way it should be." with a chime. Only at power-on, not after a game; any button skips; Nemo loads while it plays.
- A thank-you "Thank you for buying your console from NCL" for sold consoles: "NCL screen › On next start" shows it once at the next power-on, then it turns itself off.
- Quiet XMB-like interface sounds: move, select, back, tabs, list edge, menu. Silent while music plays.
- "Settings › System › Nemo": "Welcome screen" (on by default), "NCL screen" (off by default), "Interface sounds"; moving in Settings has its own sound.
- "Battery test": the standard test now runs at 222 MHz — at 333 MHz under constant load the PSP switched off after about 35 minutes.
- Music: a fourth view "All tracks" (□) — every track from MUSIC and its subfolders, A to Z.

# 1.2.2 — Battery
- Settings › About PSP › Battery: charge and status; "Battery details" — voltage, current, temperature, time left, capacity, wear, charge cycles, serial number (read only).
- "Battery test": a standard test with its own game-like load (CPU, graphics, memory, card reads, max brightness) and "Everyday use"; no Wi-Fi or sound, the PSP runs until it switches off, a log line every 10 seconds (charge, voltage, free memory). Auto sleep and backlight auto-off are off during the test and come back after it or at the next Nemo start.
- "Test results": the last 5 tests with a charge and voltage graph.
- "Nemo estimate" and "In a game" in battery details: Nemo's own time left from CPU load and brightness; Nemo refines the drain itself while running on battery.
- PSP Go and E1000: battery readings and test results may be inaccurate.

# 1.2.1 — Player
- The Square button in Music switches the view: Albums → Artists → Folders.
- Artists: albums first (newest on top), then tracks; "All tracks" lists every song of the artist.
- Folders — as on the card: MUSIC and its subfolders, tracks by file name; the Triangle menu plays the folder, with subfolders, shuffled, adds it to the queue or a playlist.
- .m3u playlists in MUSIC are imported automatically and become ordinary playlists; any playlist can be saved to MUSIC/Playlists for the PC.
- Equalizer (Now Playing › Triangle): 5 bands, presets, "Custom", on/off switch and reset.
- Cyrillic folder and file names: the PSP lists names in Shift-JIS, now they show correctly (folders, track and game titles from file names, .m3u); tags and the game list are read again once.
- The button focus ring on Now Playing has no line through the middle.
- Power window dimming on every tab; "Reset settings" is last in System again.

# 1.2.0 — Fixes and debugging
- FLAC plays (16/24-bit, mono and stereo, up to 48 kHz); an unsupported file is skipped with a note.
- Screen off: a short SELECT press wakes the screen, SELECT+L / SELECT+R switch the track without waking it.
- Power window: hold HOME for 1 s instead of 1.5 s, the background fades to dark.
- Settings › System › Debug: RAM / CPU / FPS box at the bottom left (SELECT+Triangle shows and hides it), "Keep logs": Off / 2 / 5 / 10 / 20.
- Once a minute the log records FPS, CPU clock and load, free memory.
- Bad battery readings no longer reach the indicator.
- About Nemo › Version opens this list of changes; the stage name is gone from the version line.
- New logo: the NCL "C".
- README: every button combination.
