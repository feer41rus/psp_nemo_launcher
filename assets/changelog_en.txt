# 1.3.12 — Battery and Bluetooth
- The battery icon has four cells of 25 %. While charging the cell of the current level blinks: red under 10 %, green from 90 %, grey between. Fully charged — all cells green, no blinking. On battery under 10 % — the icon is red.
- PSP Go: Network › Bluetooth — whether Bluetooth is on, what is connected and the paired devices (view only for now). While a device is connected a Bluetooth icon appears at the top and R moves aside.
- The detailed log on a PSP Go records where the console keeps Bluetooth (for the next version) and the battery icon's changes.

# 1.3.11 — Music with the screen off
- In listening mode (screen off) plain L / R switch tracks as well as SELECT+L / SELECT+R — the screen stays off.
- When music plays and the note appears at the top, the tab strip shortens smoothly and the R glyph slides left — nothing runs under the icons.
- The "Low on space" badge is a little more translucent.

# 1.3.10 — Low on space
- Home shows a "Low on space" badge with the free space under the clock, Wi‑Fi and battery when the card has under 1 GB or under 10 % free; under 200 MB the badge is red. Its background is translucent — the game's artwork shows through a little.
- On a PSP Go the badge lists both storages: Internal and M2 card.
- The badge can be turned off: Settings › System › Nemo › Warn when space runs low (on by default).
- The Wi‑Fi icon and the FTP pill at the top sit on the bar's middle, level with the clock and battery.

# 1.3.9 — Small improvements
- The battery icon at the top right sits exactly on the bar's middle, level with the clock's and percentage's digits.
- The battery percentage next to the icon can be hidden: Settings › System › Nemo › Battery percentage.
- "Delete game" right in the Triangle menu on Home and in the Library — the same "delete forever?" question, without opening the game card.
- "Screen brightness" (4 levels, a drop-down) is now right in Settings › System — it used to hide in Power saving.

# 1.3.8 — Library background
- The Library has a background under a matte coat — shelf and game titles stay readable on any picture. Six Nemo pictures about choice and possibility: Branching lives, The platform, Three lives, Leaf in the wind, Mars 2092, The League.
- Your own background: Organizer › Gallery, Triangle › "Set as Library background" — Nemo cuts the picture to the screen and adds the matte coat itself.
- The choice — Settings › System › Nemo › Library background (six pictures and Your own).
- The battery icon at the top right has rounded corners, like the rest of the interface.
- Settings with more than two values (date format, brightness, screen off, auto sleep, ARK options and others) now open a drop-down list; Left/Right still step through the values.

# 1.3.7 — Full-screen Now Playing
- Now Playing opens full screen: no tab bar, a bigger cover, the time and battery in the corner.
- L / R switch tracks here (previous / next) instead of tabs; SELECT+L / SELECT+R work as before. Circle — back to the library.

# 1.3.6 — Camera
- "Organizer › Camera" for the Go!Cam (PSP-450): a full-screen viewfinder (lens toward you — like a mirror), music paused meanwhile.
- Cross — a 640×480 photo (PICTURE/Camera) or start/stop a 480×272 video with the microphone's sound (VIDEO/Camera); Triangle — photo or video; Square — 3 or 10 s timer; L/R — zoom; SELECT — filter: Film, B&W, Sepia, Cold, Warm (viewfinder and photos).
- File names by the date and time taken. No camera — "Connect the camera", Cross to retry.

# 1.3.5 — UMD Music
- A UMD Music disc is the album "UMD: title" at the end of Music, tracks "Track 1", "Track 2"…; it plays like any music (pause, seeking, queue, screen off).
- Insert the disc — the notice "UMD Music · title", START listens from the first track (Now Playing). Take it out — the album leaves Music.
- Video and disc music one at a time: opening Video stops the UMD track.

# 1.3.4 — UMD Video: titles and sound
- In the UMD Video player Triangle has new rows: "Title" (the disc's film, trailers, extras — Left/Right to choose, Cross to watch) and "Sound" (the disc's audio tracks when there are several — switched at once).
- The chosen track is kept together with the place you stopped.

# 1.3.3 — Video
- "Organizer › Video": the videos of the VIDEO folder full screen, music paused meanwhile. MP4 films made for the PSP (H.264 Baseline/Main up to 480×272, AAC sound) in hardware; camera video — AVI and MOV with Motion JPEG (PCM, mu-law, IMA ADPCM, AAC sound).
- UMD Video discs: insert one — "UMD Video · title", START plays the film in Nemo's player; the disc is the first row of Video, the place you stopped is kept. Cross — pause, Left/Right — 10 s, L/R — a minute, Triangle — info and subtitles, Circle — back.
- "Continue from where you left off or start over?" — Nemo remembers where you stopped; a video watched to the end starts over.
- Subtitles .srt and .ass next to the video (Cyrillic — UTF-8 and Windows-1251): track, size, background, height; SELECT+Left/Right — shift by 0.1 s.
- An unsupported file gets a clear message: format, codec and why.
- "Settings › System › Debug › Detailed log" (off by default) — for beta testing: the console's passport at start, every press and screen, setting changes, game launches, video, power, a snapshot each minute; after an error Nemo suggests turning it on.
- The logs of Nemo, Control Center and the battery test now live in "PSP/NEMO/logs" (old ones moved there).

# 1.3.2 — Organizer and file manager
- The Photos tab became the Organizer: rows "Gallery" (the former Photos), "File manager", "Video" and "Camera" (the last two coming soon). Switch — "Show Organizer".
- File manager: the memory card's folders (on a PSP Go the internal storage too, Left/Right). Square marks, Triangle — copy, move, paste, delete, rename, new folder. Copying shows progress, Circle cancels; music keeps playing. ARK, SEPLUGINS and PSP/NEMO folders change only after a second question.
- Pictures open straight from the file manager (Cross) — full screen as in the Gallery: Left/Right browse that folder's pictures, Circle goes back.
- Text editor for configs (json, txt, ini…): Square edits a line with the keyboard (now with symbols), Triangle — save (asks first), find, add or delete a line; START — find next.

# 1.3.1 — Photos
- Photos tab (before Music) — a separate window opened with Cross; game artwork is unloaded meanwhile, music keeps playing: every picture on the card as thumbnails — game screenshots, Nemo screenshots, camera shots, uploads. Cross opens it full screen: Left/Right browse, R/L zoom in/out, Square rotates (saved into the file), Triangle — rotate left, delete, info. Large photos rotate in parts — with a question and a waiting screen.
- Screenshots in one place: games — "PICTURE/Nemo/Games", Nemo — "PICTURE/Nemo/Screenshots" (old ones moved).
- Faster start: Home first, the music library is read after it.
- The tab strip scrolls; switch "Show Photos" — Settings › System › Nemo.

# 1.2.6 — Memory
- "Settings › About PSP › Memory": free space on the card; "What uses the space" — on request Nemo counts how much games, DLC, saves, music, photos and video take; "Details" — the card's file system, cluster size, RAM and system flash. On a PSP Go the internal storage comes first (←→ — the card).

# 1.2.5 — DLC and UMD
- "Add-ons (DLC)" in the game card's Triangle menu — "Install DLC": DLC from the "PSP/NEMO/install_dlc" folder goes into the game's folder by itself. DLC for another version of the game can be adapted (Nemo asks). Encrypted DLC without a licence is not installed — Nemo warns. The card shows "DLC: installed".
- "Add-ons (DLC) › Installed DLC": the DLC with names and sizes, mark some and delete them — the game's and other DLC's files stay.
- Game card: format and size ("ISO · 865 MB", "PSN", "CSO", "EBOOT"); "Delete game" asks "for good".
- UMD discs: insert a disc — a "UMD inserted" note, START plays it right away; the disc becomes the main game on Home and shows in the Library marked "UMD".
- UMD Video and UMD Music are not supported yet (a note when inserted).

# 1.2.4 — Control Center
- Control Center: in PSP games and homebrew, HOME opens Nemo's window instead of the Sony one — Resume, Screenshot, Volume, Brightness, Quit game (with a question); the clock and charge on top. The game stands still and silent while it is open.
- Game screenshots go to PICTURE/Nemo (shown in XMB › Photo).
- Nemo installs and enables the plugin itself; the switch is Settings › System › Nemo › Control Center.
- Plugin manager: what the selected plugin does, under the list.

# 1.2.3 — Splash screens and sounds
- Splash screen at power-on: "Welcome! Your PSP. The way it should be." with a chime. Only at power-on, not after a game; any button skips; Nemo loads while it plays.
- A thank-you "Thank you for buying your console from NCL" for sold consoles: "NCL screen › On next start" shows it once at the next power-on, then it turns itself off.
- Quiet XMB-like interface sounds: move, select, back, tabs, list edge, menu. Silent while music plays.
- "Settings › System › Nemo": "Welcome screen" (on by default), "NCL screen" (off by default), "Interface sounds"; moving in Settings has its own sound.
- "Battery test": the standard test now runs at 222 MHz — at 333 MHz under constant load the PSP switched off after about 35 minutes.
- Music: a fourth view "All tracks" (□) — every track from MUSIC and its subfolders, A to Z.

# 1.2.2 — Battery
- Settings › About PSP › Battery: charge and status; "Battery details" — voltage, current, temperature, time left, capacity, wear, charge cycles, serial number (read only).
- "Battery test": a standard test with its own game-like load (CPU, graphics, memory, card reads, max brightness) and "Everyday use"; no Wi-Fi or sound, the PSP runs until it switches off, a log line every 10 seconds (charge, voltage, free memory). Auto sleep and backlight auto-off are off during the test and come back after it or at the next Nemo start.
- "Test results": the last 5 tests with a charge and voltage graph.
- "Nemo estimate" and "In a game" in battery details: Nemo's own time left from CPU load and brightness; Nemo refines the drain itself while running on battery.
- PSP Go and E1000: battery readings and test results may be inaccurate.

# 1.2.1 — Player
- The Square button in Music switches the view: Albums → Artists → Folders.
- Artists: albums first (newest on top), then tracks; "All tracks" lists every song of the artist.
- Folders — as on the card: MUSIC and its subfolders, tracks by file name; the Triangle menu plays the folder, with subfolders, shuffled, adds it to the queue or a playlist.
- .m3u playlists in MUSIC are imported automatically and become ordinary playlists; any playlist can be saved to MUSIC/Playlists for the PC.
- Equalizer (Now Playing › Triangle): 5 bands, presets, "Custom", on/off switch and reset.
- Cyrillic folder and file names: the PSP lists names in Shift-JIS, now they show correctly (folders, track and game titles from file names, .m3u); tags and the game list are read again once.
- The button focus ring on Now Playing has no line through the middle.
- Power window dimming on every tab; "Reset settings" is last in System again.
